Included

Scene editor

Entity hierarchy, transforms, scene save/load, viewport controls and a compact inspector.

VOIDBOUND

3D arena-survival prototype with enemy waves, movement, dash, projectiles, XP and upgrades.

NEXUS scripting

Edit and compile GameRules.nx from the editor. Balance values control waves, enemies, scoring and upgrades.

LICMAT materials

Reusable, versioned text materials with base color, metallic, roughness and emission. Scene files preserve surface settings.

Project status

The Vulkan viewport currently renders primitive cube geometry. LICMAT surface parameters work with that renderer; imported glTF models, texture maps, full PBR lighting, general physics, and mobile/Web packages are still in development.
